ABOUT THE HISTORY

The romance of Sanyogita and Prithviraj comes from the Prithviraj Raso, whose surviving versions were written down centuries after the events. The Prithviraja Vijaya, composed in Prithviraj's lifetime, does not mention her in the part that survives, and no inscription names her, so historians disagree on whether she lived. Prithviraj, Jayachandra (Jaichand) of the Gahadavala dynasty, his son Harishchandra, and the battles of Tarain (1192) and Chandawar (1194) are all well attested.
