# ice-cream-melts

## Hook (max 3 s)
"Your ice cream was never fully frozen." Title card on screen from frame 0, ice cream sticker.

## Beats
1. Four ingredients: ice crystals, air bubbles, fat, sugary syrup. Card with 4 lines popping on each word.
2. Sugar lowers the freezing point. BigNumber counts to -2.5 °C, "not 0 °C" sticker.
3. Freezer at about -18 °C: some syrup is still liquid, which is why it scoops. Card + spoon sticker.
4. Warm up: ice crystals melt back to water. Title "ICE -> WATER".

## Twist
Melting ice is only half the story: what drips is a foam collapsing. A net of fat clumps around air bubbles holds the shape. Strong net = holds, weak net = puddle. Stabilizers keep crystals small, so it melts slower.
